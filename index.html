BOUNTY RULES

Objective

Be the last player standing or hit an Instant Win in a Bounty Challenge.

Setup

Deck 52-card deck with 4 colors. Aces are low, Kings are high.
First Player All players flip 1 card. The lowest rank goes first. Play proceeds clockwise.
Up Card Shuffle the deck and flip the first card to create the first Up Card.

Gameplay

Normal Turn Guess if the next card from the deck is higher or lower than the current Up Card.
Correct The flipped card becomes the new Up Card and your turn ends.
Incorrect Add the flipped card to your hand and your turn ends.
Tie Re-draw. You may change your guess.
Empty Deck Keep the Up Card. Shuffle the discard pile to create a new draw pile.

Elimination & Combos

You are eliminated if you hold 4 cards at the end of your turn, unless you can form a discard combo.
Pair 2 cards of the same rank.
3-Card Straight 3 cards in sequence (e.g., 4-5-6).
3-Card Flush 3 cards of the same color.
Combo Play Discard these immediately and end your turn.

Hand Actions

Play these before you make your guess.

4 Sabotage: Skip your turn. The next player must guess the card color.
J Shield: Skip your turn. The next player must guess Higher/Lower.

Up Card Effects

These trigger at the start of your turn.

3 Triple: You must guess correctly for 3 cards in a row.
7 7: A correct guess earns you a discard.
Q Queen (Rainbow): Starting with you, players guess the color of the next card. This continues until a player guesses correctly, or until the Queen is covered by a discard or a Hand Action.
K King (Bounty Challenge): If you hold a card, you may accept or decline the challenge. If you accept, flip one card for every card in your hand. If you match any rank, you win instantly. If you fail, take the last card and end your turn. If you decline, play a standard turn.
